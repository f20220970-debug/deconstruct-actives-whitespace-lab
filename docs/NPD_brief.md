# NPD Brief (draft): Gentle Centella Calming Serum

**Prepared by:** Pavitra Seth · **Status:** proposal for discussion · **Evidence:** this repo

## 1. Why this concept
- **Demand signal:** centella products average 4.23 stars; calming and barrier care are where shoppers
  reward gentle formulas (ceramides are the one active with a clearly positive rating link, +0.13 stars).
- **Supply gap:** only **12 gentle** (fragrance-free, no drying alcohol) centella products among 1,231
  formulas; 72% of centella products carry fragrance, allergens or essential oils, which contradicts the
  "calming" promise.
- **Brand fit:** sits next to Deconstruct's ceramide line (Skin Soothe Moisturizer) as a serum step, and
  pairs with actives users already buy (niacinamide, salicylic acid) as the "calm it down" product.
- **To confirm:** I didn't find a centella product on Deconstruct's site in a quick check.

## 2. Target consumer and job to be done
Indian 20–35 year-olds using actives (acids, retinoids, niacinamide) who get redness or stinging:
*"Help my skin recover so I can keep using my actives."*

## 3. Product hypothesis (for R&D to challenge)
- Hero: centella asiatica extract plus madecassoside, with panthenol and a low-% ceramide complex.
- Free from: fragrance, essential oils, drying alcohol. Percentages disclosed on pack (brand standard).
- Texture: light, quick-absorbing serum for humid climates (gentle formulas lose ~0.05 stars on average,
  so texture has to make up for the missing fragrance).
- Price: in line with the current serum range.

## 4. Claims to test
1. "Calms visible redness in X days" (needs an instrumental or dermatologist-graded study).
2. "Safe to use with your actives" (compatibility and user trial).
3. "Fragrance-free, 0% essential oils, every percentage on the pack" (trust claim; no study needed).

## 5. Research plan
| Step | Method | Size | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Listen | Mine reviews of top centella and barrier products on Nykaa and Amazon India (same method as this repo, in English and Hinglish) | 2–5K reviews | Top complaints: stinging, pilling, smell, price |
| 2. Ask | 10–12 consumer interviews with active users who report irritation | 12 | Language, routines, triggers |
| 3. Test the concept | Monadic concept test: control vs 3 variants (claim and price) | **547 per concept** (scorecard workbook) | Top-2-box purchase intent, preferred claim |
| 4. Test the product | Home-use test of 2 prototypes, 2 weeks | 60–100 users | Liking, tolerance, repurchase intent |

## 6. Success metrics and gates
- Concept test: top-2-box purchase intent at least 8 points above control.
- Home-use test: at least 80% would repurchase; under 5% report stinging.
- Launch KPIs: repeat rate at 60 days, rating at or above 4.3 on marketplaces, share of buyers who also own a
  Deconstruct active (cross-sell).

## 7. Risks
- Centella is crowded in K-beauty imports; the edge has to be disclosure plus compatibility with actives.
- Extract quality varies by supplier: R&D to benchmark 2–3 vendors (part of the R&D intern scope).
